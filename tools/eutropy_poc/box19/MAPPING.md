## Units
- Concentrations: mg/L (g/m^3); AKI_C is g/m^2.
- `Cpy`->`DIA_C` is carbon->carbon (no conversion). Chlorophyll comparison uses AQUABC's `DIA_C_TO_CHLA` constant.

## Provenance / caveats
- Forcing: 1827 daily means from EUTROPY box 19 (base date 2012-01-01); temperature/salinity/light/daylight are real, air-temp is proxied by water temp, wind/elevation/bg-extinction/ice are constants (see script header).
- Boundary: time-averaged `Nemunas` concentrations as a fixed BC. A faithful box-19 BC would be the flux-weighted mix of its neighbours.
- The 21 AQUABC-only variables are seeded from the Curonian 0-D defaults, NOT from EUTROPY. Review `AQUABC_DEFAULTS` in the script.
- Constants are NOT converted; reuse `const_CL.txt`.
